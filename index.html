How I work

Three habits that survive every reorg.

01

Model the decision, not the data.

Start from the choice someone has to make. The schema and the dashboard follow from that, not the other way round.

02

One metric, one definition.

If two dashboards disagree, neither gets trusted. Governance is what makes self-service safe.

03

Automate the last mile.

An insight only counts when it reaches the person who acts on it — ideally before they think to ask.
