How do you report cross-border wallet growth without a nightly scramble?

The situation

The international wallet business ran on cross-border money transfer volumes and user engagement, with reporting expected every quarter. The data existed, but the path from wallet events to a finished report depended on when pipelines had last run and whether the reporting layer was current.

When business teams asked a question, the answer required a translation step between them and the technical team — and a report assembled under time pressure is a report with quiet caveats. The fix was structural: define the ingestion, protect the layer, then report from it.

Approach

  1. 01

    Model the wallet

    Design SQL models for users, transfers, corridors and engagement so the quarterly questions map to known tables rather than ad-hoc extracts.

  2. 02

    Make ingestion explicit

    Manage pipeline ingestion intervals and treat the availability of the reporting layer as a requirement — not something discovered at close.

  3. 03

    Automate the pack

    Produce quarterly performance reporting and dashboards on cross-border transfer volumes and user engagement from a stable template.

  4. 04

    Bridge the two sides

    Managed local BI staff and acted as the primary liaison between business departments and technical teams, translating the question before it became a ticket.
