Which ad actually produced the lead?

The situation

Traffic and lead generation ran through web and app funnels, measured in Google Analytics 4 and SQL. Spend lived in the ad platform. Revenue lived in the internal database. Each system was internally consistent and collectively useless for the one question marketing needed answered: what did that spend return?

Platform-reported conversions flattered the platform. Last-click flattered branded search. Finance and marketing were looking at different numbers and calling it a disagreement about strategy.

Approach

  1. 01

    Instrument the funnel

    Analyse web and app traffic and lead-generation funnels in GA4 and SQL, and name the drop-off points that were worth fixing.

  2. 02

    Join at the right grain

    Bring Google Ads spend and internal revenue into one model at campaign-by-date grain, with the definitions written down before the first chart.

  3. 03

    Give marketing a ROAS view

    An attribution model that assigns credit, and a view where a channel's spend, leads and revenue sit on the same line — built for an ad-spend decision, not a report.

  4. 04

    Validate, don't assert

    Test feature and campaign impact with A/B testing and historical trend analysis, so the model's claims could be checked against behaviour.
