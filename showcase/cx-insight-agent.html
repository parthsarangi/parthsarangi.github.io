How do you notice a contact-volume spike before it becomes a backlog?

The situation

Customer experience at marketplace scale runs on three channels with three different rhythms. Chat spikes when a campaign lands. Voice spikes when deliveries slip. Both spike again when a payment gate fails. In a weekly review, all of that arrives after the queue has already formed.

The analytics team had the data — chat and voice logs, contact reasons, logistics events, customer feedback — but the read was manual and periodic. The gap was not modelling. It was the last mile: getting the interpretation to the operations lead while it still mattered.

Approach

  1. 01

    Define the signal

    Set one KPI framework across chatbot, voicebot and human agents, with data-quality standards for interaction logs so the three channels could be compared at all.

  2. 02

    Automate the read

    AI agents generate the daily data insight and executive summary, replacing a manual sweep of contact volume, contact reasons and resolution metrics.

  3. 03

    Alert on the exception

    Push alerts fire when chat or voice volume breaks its normal band — the moment an operations lead needs to reallocate, not the morning after.

  4. 04

    Predict the next spike

    Logistics delays and customer feedback feed models that forecast contact-volume spikes, enabling proactive customer communication before the queue builds.
